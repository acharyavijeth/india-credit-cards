How to Apply for Credit Cards

A complete guide to credit card applications, eligibility, and pro tips from India's top finance creators

1

Check Eligibility

  • Minimum age: 21 years (18 for add-on cards)
  • Stable income source (salaried or self-employed)
  • Good CIBIL score (750+ preferred, 700+ minimum)
  • KYC documents: PAN, Aadhaar, salary slips
  • Last 3 months bank statements
  • Form 16 or ITR for self-employed
2

Apply at Your Salary Bank First

  • Your salary bank sees your income — 3x higher approval rate
  • Pre-approved offers often waive joining fees
  • Check NetBanking/app for pre-approved card offers
  • HDFC, ICICI, Axis, SBI — all push offers to salary holders
  • Existing relationship = better card limits
3

Where to Apply

  • Bank website/app — best for pre-approved offers
  • Paisabazaar / BankBazaar — compare & apply in one place
  • Bank branch — for premium cards needing relationship
  • Referral links — often get bonus joining benefits
  • Amex Platinum — referrals from existing holders give 50K+ bonus
4

Pro Tips from Creators

  • Never apply for multiple cards at once — each hard inquiry drops CIBIL by 5-10 points
  • Always negotiate annual fee waiver — call retention desk before renewal
  • Convert large purchases to no-cost EMI — keeps utilization below 30%
  • Pay full bill before due date — never pay just minimum amount
  • Aly Hajiani: Stack 2-3 cards for different categories (online, dining, travel)
5

Building Credit Score

  • Start with a lifetime-free card (Amazon Pay ICICI, IDFC Classic)
  • Use 30-50% of credit limit consistently for 6 months
  • Pay full bill on or before due date
  • After 6-12 months, apply for premium cards
  • Don't close old cards — credit history length matters
6

Avoid These Mistakes

  • Withdrawing cash from credit card — high charges + interest from day 1
  • Paying only minimum due — interest compounds at 36-42% annually
  • Maxing out credit limit — hurts CIBIL score
  • Closing first credit card — reduces credit history age
  • Ignoring add-on card terms — same liability as primary
