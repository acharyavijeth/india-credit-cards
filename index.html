How to Apply for Credit Cards

1

Check Eligibility

  • Minimum age: 21 years (18 for add-on cards)
  • Stable income source (salaried or self-employed)
  • Good CIBIL score (750+ preferred, 700+ minimum)
  • KYC documents: PAN, Aadhaar, salary slips
2

Apply at Your Salary Bank First

  • Your salary bank sees your income — 3x higher approval rate
  • Pre-approved offers often waive joining fees
  • Check NetBanking/app for pre-approved card offers
  • HDFC, ICICI, Axis, SBI — all push offers to salary account holders
3

Where to Apply

  • Bank website/app — best for pre-approved offers
  • Paisabazaar / BankBazaar — compare & apply in one place
  • Bank branch — for premium cards needing relationship
  • Referral links — often get bonus joining benefits
4

Pro Tips from Creators

  • Never apply for multiple cards at once — each hard inquiry drops CIBIL by 5-10 points
  • Always negotiate annual fee waiver — call retention desk before renewal
  • Convert large purchases to no-cost EMI to maintain utilization ratio below 30%
  • Pay full bill before due date — never pay just minimum amount
